# Polaris Twin — Station SOPs (Maitri and Bharati)

## 1. Field travel
Limits (any one stops the trip):
- Wind above 40 km/h, wind chill below -30 °C, or visibility under 1 km.
- Field safety index below 70 = caution, below 40 = no-go.
- Minimum team of 2 (buddy rule). Trips over 10 h need an approved camp plan.

Check-out:
1. Log the destination, route, team names, departure time and expected return in the field book.
2. Carry a VHF radio, satellite phone, GPS, first-aid kit, survival bag and food for 24 h.
3. Radio the station every 60 minutes.

Overdue:
1. 30 min past a missed radio call: the station calls the team on VHF and satellite phone.
2. 60 min overdue: the station leader is informed and the search team gets ready.
3. 90 min overdue: start the person-missing procedure (section 8).

## 2. Generator warning signs and switchover
Warning signs: coolant above 95 °C, vibration above 5 mm/s, load above 85% of 250 kW, a sudden rise in fuel burn, smoke or a new noise.
Critical: coolant above 105 °C or vibration above 8 mm/s.

1. On a warning, start the standby generator and let it warm up for 5 minutes.
2. Synchronise it and move load across until the faulty set carries less than 30%.
3. Take the faulty set offline and let it cool before opening anything.
4. Check coolant level, radiator, fan belt, mounts and bearings; log the readings.
5. On a critical reading, switch over immediately and shed non-essential load first.

## 3. Fuel reserve and conservation
- Margin = days of diesel left at the current burn rate minus days until the ISEA ship arrives.
- Plan for at least 30 days of margin. Below 30 days: amber. Below 10 days: red.

Amber:
1. Lower non-essential heating set-points by 2 °C.
2. Run one generator where load allows and use battery and renewables first.
3. Report the runway to NCPOR Goa every week.

Red:
1. Close unused buildings and labs and drain their water lines.
2. Suspend vehicle use except for safety.
3. Report the runway to NCPOR Goa every day and request a fuel transfer or an earlier ship.

## 4. Blizzard
Triggers: wind above 60 km/h or visibility under 1 km.
1. Announce the blizzard on the public address system and stop all outdoor work.
2. Recall field teams; teams that cannot return shelter in place and report every 30 min.
3. Fit guide ropes between buildings; nobody moves outside alone.
4. Check the comms mast and fuel lines once the wind drops below 40 km/h.

## 5. Fire
1. Raise the alarm and announce the location.
2. Fight the fire with an extinguisher only if it is small and the exit is behind you.
3. Isolate fuel and electricity to the affected building.
4. Muster at the designated point and take a head count within 5 minutes.
5. Keep the fire team at the scene; protect the fuel farm and the power house first.

## 6. Medical evacuation
1. The medical officer stabilises the patient and records vital signs.
2. The station leader informs NCPOR Goa and requests medical advice by satellite link.
3. If evacuation is needed, agree the route (helicopter, aircraft or ship) and the weather window.
4. Prepare the patient, records and medicines for the move; keep a crew member with them.

## 7. Power failure
1. Battery backup carries critical loads (comms, medical, heating controls) for at least 30 min.
2. Start the standby generator; restore power to living, medical and comms first.
3. If no generator can run within 1 hour, move the crew to the warmest building and close the others.
4. Find the cause before reconnecting the failed set; log the event.

## 8. Person missing
1. Sound the general alarm and take a head count.
2. Collect last known position, time, clothing and equipment.
3. Search the station buildings first, then send search teams of 3 or more with radios.
4. Inform NCPOR Goa within 1 hour; request help from neighbouring stations if needed.

## 9. Waste and spills (Madrid Protocol)
The Protocol on Environmental Protection to the Antarctic Treaty (Madrid Protocol) applies.
- Sort all waste at source; no open burning.
- Burn only approved waste in the incinerator; pack the rest for return on the ship.
- Never release fuel, oil or chemicals to snow, ice, lakes or the sea.

Spills:
1. Stop the source and isolate ignition sources.
2. Contain the spill with absorbent pads and booms from the spill kit.
3. Collect contaminated snow and soil into sealed drums for removal.
4. Log the volume, location and clean-up in the compliance register and report it to NCPOR.

## 10. Water supply
- Maitri: fresh water is pumped from Priyadarshini Lake. Keep the intake line heated; check it twice a day in winter.
- Bharati: water comes from the station's water treatment plant.
- Keep at least 20,000 L in storage. Below that level, increase pumping or snow melting and limit showers and laundry.
- Test drinking water every week and log the results.
